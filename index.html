Credibility & Recognition

Their excellence becomes your qualification

Nirman Campus works in affiliation with MRSPTU, Bathinda and is an IGNOU Study Centre. Studying here means inheriting the stature of two of India's most trusted universities.

#1NIRF RANK — OPEN UNIVERSITY CATEGORY
Government of India

IGNOU — Central Open University

IGNOU is a Central University run by the Government of India, holding NIRF Rank #1 in the Open University category. It is recognised by UGC and is the largest open university in India. From this study centre, we offer the same programmes that flow from that NIRF #1-ranked university — with approvals from UGC, DEB, AIU, AICTE and the Ministry of Education, valid in India and abroad.

A++NAAC ACCREDITATION
Quality-assessed academic standards

MRSPTU — Statutory Technical University

Maharaja Ranjit Singh Punjab Technical University, Bathinda, established under Punjab Act No. 5 of 2015, anchors your technical and professional pathway. Our programmes align with NAAC evaluation standards, and the affiliation delivers rigorous scientific and professional training designed for real workplace readiness.
